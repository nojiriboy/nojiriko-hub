16-Week Portland-to-Nagano Roadmap

Phase 1 • Weeks 1–4 Leif Erikson Drive

Initiation, Medical Baseline & Tendon Priming

  • Medical & Nutrition: Initiate GLP-1/GIP titration. Lock in 160–210g daily protein (4 boluses of 35–45g) + daily hydration & PEG 3350/methylcellulose fiber.
  • Aerobic Base: 3–4x weekly flat gravel walks on Leif Erikson Drive (30–45 mins in conversational FatMax Zone 2) for mitochondrial biogenesis without joint shear.
  • Tendon & Strength: 25° decline eccentric single-leg squats (2–3 sets × 10–12 reps, 3–5s lowering, push up on 2 legs) + light farmer's carries & Romanian deadlifts 2x/wk.
Phase 2 • Weeks 5–8 Wild Cherry / Alder Loop

Dose Escalation & Uneven Proprioception

  • Proprioceptive Trails: Transition from flat gravel to dirt single-tracks with roots, mud, and undulating grades (Wild Cherry & Alder trails in Forest Park, 60 mins).
  • Footwear Break-In: Acquire and break in rigid, deep-lugged boots (Salomon Quest 5 GTX or La Sportiva Ultra Raptor II Mid GTX).
  • Strength Progression: Add 10–15 lb dumbbell/plate to 25° decline squats. Introduce rotational cable woodchoppers for torso/oblique conditioning.
Phase 3 • Weeks 9–12 Mount Tabor Stairs

Vertical Stair Capacity & Early Load Carriage

  • Vertical Ascents: Shift 1–2 cardio sessions/week to Mount Tabor Park stair climbs (up to 280 steps). Focus on steady rhythmic breathing and glute drive.
  • Rucking Introduction: Add a 15–20 lb weighted backpack to one weekly trail hike to adapt axial skeleton and postural stabilizers.
  • Heavy Carries: Progress trap-bar deadlifts, heavy farmer's carries, and asymmetric suitcase carries (Pallof presses).
Phase 4 • Weeks 13–16 Full Cabin Simulation

500m Haul Simulation, Ax Power & Cold Priming

  • 500m Cabin Haul: Execute 500-meter loaded carries on mixed grades holding 25–35 lbs (backpack + duffels) in broken-in boots.
  • Firewood Mechanics: Explosive cable chops, sledgehammer tire slams, or rotational medicine ball throws for lats, grip, and obliques.
  • Thermal Adaptation: Finish daily showers with 1–2 minutes of cold water to stimulate brown adipose tissue (BAT) and blunt cold-induced bronchospasm.

Patellar Tendon Shield: 25° Decline Squat

Mandatory 2x/Wk

Walking down steep mud or snow slopes in Nojiri-ko places massive eccentric braking forces on the patellar tendon. Using a 25-degree slant board reduces calf tension and isolates the knee extensor mechanism, stimulating collagen cross-linking without exceeding safe patellofemoral cartilage compression limits.

1. Eccentric Down (1 Leg)

Stand on 25° decline board on ONE leg. Lower slowly over 3 to 6 seconds with torso upright.

2. Strict <60° Angle Cap

Stop at 45°–60° knee flexion. Deeper angles spike retropatellar shear at 295 lbs.

3. Concentric Up (2 Legs)

Place your other foot down and push back to the top using BOTH legs. Perform 3×12 reps.

In head-to-head trials (SURMOUNT vs. STEP), dual GIP/GLP-1 agonist Tirzepatide (Zepbound) achieved ~20.9% mean weight reduction vs ~14.9% for single-agonist Semaglutide (Wegovy), with steeper early-phase loss in Weeks 1–16 (~10–12% body weight or ~28–35 lbs from a 295 lb baseline).

GLP-1 Gastrointestinal & Muscle Protection Rules

1. Hydration Anchor (3.0L+/day)

Drink 16–20 oz water immediately upon waking and before each protein bolus to maintain preload and bowel transit.

2. Motility Support (PEG 3350)

Use non-fermentable soluble fiber (Citrucel/methylcellulose) + osmotic PEG 3350 (MiraLAX) as needed during dose escalations.

3. Avoid High-Fat Boluses

GLP-1s slow gastric emptying. Greasy/fried meals sit in the stomach and trigger sulfur burps, reflux, or nausea.

4. Liquid Protein Fallback

On days when solid meat feels too heavy, use whey isolate, Fairlife shakes, egg whites, and 0% Greek yogurt to hit 180g.
